## STYLE LOCK — prepend to every prompt
Create original modern high-detail pixel-art game assets for “Ao Làng”, a northern Vietnamese village pond. Use deliberate connected pixel clusters, clear silhouettes and strong readable volumes, not isolated confetti pixels or repetitive texture stamps. One coherent warm late-afternoon light from upper left; cool deep-teal occlusion shadows, moss/olive foliage, weathered terracotta, muted gold stone highlights. Consistent3/4 top-down game camera with a modest view of top surfaces, no fisheye or perspective lens effects. Designed for a640×360game canvas and shown at exact2× in a1280×720PNG. Crisp square pixel edges and restrained palette, no smooth gradients, blur, glow, photorealism, vector-flat shapes, text, watermark, UI or borders. Keep northern Vietnamese identity; no Japanese torii, Chinese palace, European oak, fantasy castle or tropical resort. Do not copy assets from an existing game. All water animation and reflections will be generated separately by code.
