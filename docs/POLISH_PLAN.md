## Guardrails and decision

The live water contract still samples the current `renderObjects(time, wind)` output. The authoritative binary water mask is unchanged; within it water pixels are opaque and use the ten RGB entries of the selected palette, and outside it output pixels are transparent. Water-only palette presets are not whole-scene lighting. Pause, simulation-time identity, effect independence, bounded rain/ripples, and integer nearest-neighbor export remain verification requirements.

The technical and image checks establish that the bounded treatment behaves as specified; they do **not** establish an aesthetic win or user acceptance. Both 640×360 and 960×540 remain comparison options. Ask the user to review the final captures and approve the look, especially the restrained visible motion and incomplete articulation where the plate does not expose a clean lotus stem.
